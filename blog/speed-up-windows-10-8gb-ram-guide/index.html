Method 1: Optimize Windows Visual Effects

One of the fastest ways to speed up your PC is to disable unnecessary visual effects.

How to Disable Visual Effects Manually

  1. Press Windows Key + R, type sysdm.cpl, and press Enter.
  2. Go to the Advanced tab and click Settings under Performance.
  3. Select "Adjust for best performance" to disable all effects.
  4. Click Apply and OK.

This simple tweak can instantly make your PC feel snappier.
